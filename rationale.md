Written Rationale
This dataset was chosen because it provided player names , the team they were on, and the amount of points they scored, allowing the user and dashboard to answer questions such as which players scored the most points, how the top point totals were distrubuted, and how players copared to others in terms of their rank in who scored the most last basketball season. 
One limitation in this app is that the dataset only focuses on points and does not include other statistical factors such as assists, rebounds, shooting efficiency, etc. Because of this, a player with more points may not determine that they are a more effective player overall. Also, another limitation is that this dataset could easily be expanded, but isn't, allowing a smaller advantage of filtering the data.
The summary metrics were selected because they describe directly the filtered data the user wanted. "Number of Players" shows how many players meet the user's criteria  and "Total Points" shows the combined points from all players that fit under the user's criteria as well. These metrics change with the filters, making them useful for understanding when data is filtered.
Lastly, the points distribution bar chart was chosen because it clearly showed the frequency in how many times a point total was obtained. The top players chart uses a dot plot to compare individual point totals, showing a clear difference easily identifiable between different players. Together, the charts show both the overall point distribution and individual player performance.
